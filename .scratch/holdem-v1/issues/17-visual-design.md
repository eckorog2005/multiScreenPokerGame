# 17: Card art and visual design

Status: resolved
Blocked by: 11, 12

## What

Make the Game look good: card faces and backs, chips, the felt table, and how much space the controls get. It has to read well on a TV across a room and on a phone in someone's hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Card faces and backs are readable at TV distance and at phone size, with suits that can be told apart without relying on color alone.
- [x] The Table layout works at 16:9 (TV) and on laptop screens.
- [x] The Player Device layout works one-handed on small phones (~360 px wide), with tap targets at least 44 px.
- [x] Any asset licenses are recorded in the repo.

## Notes

This is marked ready-for-human because it depends on taste. An agent can implement a design once one is chosen.

## Comments

Implemented in #25 with the Paper design. No outside assets: Georgia from the system font stack and text-glyph suits, so there are no licenses to record. Table (16:9, laptop) and phone (~360 px) layouts were not checked on real screens.
